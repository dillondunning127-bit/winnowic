COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know

Quick answers about Winnowic SAT Math prep.

Is Winnowic free? +

Yes. The SAT Math diagnostic, personalized study plan, daily practice batches, and streak tracking are all completely free. No account is required to take the diagnostic. Winnowic Premium ($6/month) unlocks 500+ hard SAT Math questions, adaptive practice that targets your weakest units, advanced diagnostics, and full 44-question SAT simulations.

How does the SAT Math diagnostic work? +

The free diagnostic is a 20-question test covering all major SAT Math units: Algebra, Advanced Math, Problem Solving & Data Analysis, and Geometry & Trigonometry. After completing it, you get a predicted SAT Math score range and a unit-by-unit accuracy breakdown showing exactly where you are losing points. It takes about 20 minutes and requires no account.

How many SAT Math questions does Winnowic have? +

Winnowic has 500+ SAT Math practice questions across all four SAT Math domains. Free users have access to a limited practice bank. Winnowic Premium ($6/month) unlocks the full question bank including hard difficulty questions across every unit.

What SAT Math topics does Winnowic cover? +

Winnowic covers all four SAT Math domains:

Algebra — linear equations, systems of equations, inequalities, linear functions
Advanced Math — quadratics, polynomials, exponential functions, function notation
Problem Solving & Data Analysis — ratios, percentages, statistics, scatter plots, data interpretation
Geometry & Trigonometry — angles, triangles, circles, right triangles, trigonometric ratios

How is Winnowic different from Khan Academy SAT prep? +

Both are free options for SAT Math prep. Winnowic focuses specifically on adaptive practice that targets your weakest units, a predicted score range that updates as you practice, and a personalized week-by-week study plan with daily question batches and full SAT Math simulations. Khan Academy offers broader SAT prep including Reading and Writing sections. Winnowic Premium adds 500+ hard SAT Math questions and advanced diagnostics for $6/month.

How accurate is the SAT score prediction? +

Winnowic provides a predicted SAT Math score range (e.g. 580–620) based on your accuracy across all units. The range updates every 20 questions answered and becomes more precise as you answer more questions. It is designed to give a realistic estimate of your current scoring range — not a guaranteed score — and helps track your improvement over time.
